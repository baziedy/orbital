PROJECT NAME
ORBITAL — SPHEREx Archive Explorer

SUMMARY (paste into Summary; 50–200 words)
ORBITAL is an independent, browser-based practice workspace for exploring released SPHEREx archive products and learning how to inspect astronomical images. Users can search NASA/IPAC IRSA records, preview available FITS products, browse curated Digitized Sky Survey reference fields, compare image views, and keep browser-local research notes. Educational tools illustrate parallax and orbital assumptions, while source labels explain what each image or calculation can and cannot establish. The project addresses the gap between finding scientific data and interpreting it responsibly: it brings discovery, visual review, and provenance into one approachable workflow. ORBITAL is important as a learning and exploration aid, not as a scientific detection pipeline. Curated reference images are not SPHEREx observations; comparison is display-level, and illustrative model outputs are not measurements or discoveries.

PROJECT INFORMATION
Project Details (paste into Project Details)
ORBITAL is an independent educational prototype inspired by the Planet X and SPHEREx challenge context. Its Explore workspace searches NASA/IPAC IRSA for released SPHEREx records and available FITS products, and it also presents curated STScI Digitized Sky Survey reference images for selected practice targets. Users can inspect metadata and image previews, compare views with slider/blink/side-by-side modes, browse sky layers through CDS Aladin Lite, and maintain browser-local research notes with text export. Additional tools demonstrate idealized parallax/orbit relationships, coordinate offsets, image marking, and illustrative spectral sonification. The interface uses Next.js, React, TypeScript, Tailwind/Sass, and Recharts; the service layer uses FastAPI, Python, Astropy, and Pillow. The creative approach combines archive access with a guided visual-review workflow and explicit provenance labels. Key constraints are built into the interpretation: the curated DSS dates are illustrative, comparisons are not WCS-registered or calibrated, generated spectral curves are not measured spectra, and the simulator is not a detection or orbit-fitting system. My Research data is stored locally in the browser. ORBITAL is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA or NASA Space Apps. NASA does not endorse any non-U.S. Government entity and is not responsible for information contained on non-U.S. Government websites.
